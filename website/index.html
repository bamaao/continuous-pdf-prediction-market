Two prices at once

Probability and risk capital

Unlike YES/NO markets, each listing keeps a density f(x) with ∫f = 1. Traders buy a set S; risk LPs underwrite the gap when payouts exceed trading revenue.

Prediction

Discover P(X∈S) — the probability mass on an interval, score cell, election atom, or YES. Hit pays 1 USDC per share (then × market-wide ρ); miss pays 0.

Price = current mass on S · LMSR

Risk capital

Auction discovers the cost of underwriting shortfalls when final liability exceeds trading revenue. LPs lock capital, may be drawn, and share surplus when ρ = 1.

Coverage for L when T < L

Core business flow

From family choice to surplus split

One locked lifecycle for every listing. Prediction trading and the risk auction open together, freeze together at close_ts, then resolve through committee report and a single ρ.

Soft solvency · settlement cases

ρ keeps every winner equal

One market-wide recovery rate is locked before any claim. Winners receive ⌊ρ · face⌋. VOID / RESOLUTION_FAILED refund cost_paid and do not use ρ.

Locked before claim

ρ = min(1, Cmax/L)

Cmax = T + CRfinal + CPalloc — then applied uniformly to every winning share.

Funds vs L ρ Winners get
L ≤ T 1 Full face; no risk draw
T < L ≤ Cmax 1 Full face; draw CR then CP
L > Cmax Cmax/L Same ratio for all — no FIFO

Five parametric families

Pick the shape that fits the question

The family is locked at create and cannot change later — so liquidity, scoring, and resolution stay coherent through the full lifecycle.

Skellam Football / score differentials and integer margins.
Gaussian CPI prints, rates, and other symmetric continuous outcomes.
Lognormal Same-day BTC / ETH and other positive skewed paths.
Dirichlet Elections and multi-atom categorical outcomes.
Bernoulli Deadline YES / NO with continuous PDF pricing.
